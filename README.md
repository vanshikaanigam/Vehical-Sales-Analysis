# 🚗📊 Vehicle Sales Data Analysis



# 🚀 Project Overview

The automotive industry generates vast amounts of sales data that can be leveraged to understand customer preferences, market trends, and business performance. This project focuses on analyzing vehicle sales data to uncover actionable insights that support strategic decision-making and revenue growth.

This project represents a complete, industry-standard, end-to-end data analytics workflow designed to mirror real-world business analysis processes. From data cleaning and transformation to visualization and reporting, the project demonstrates practical data analytics skills using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

## 🎯 Business Problem Statement

Vehicle dealerships and automotive companies need data-driven insights to understand sales performance, customer preferences, and market demand. Without proper analysis, identifying top-performing vehicle categories, sales trends, and profitable markets becomes challenging.

This project aims to analyze vehicle sales data to identify key revenue drivers, customer buying patterns, and sales trends that can help businesses optimize inventory management, marketing strategies, and overall profitability.

---

## 📌 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall vehicle sales performance.
* Identify top-selling vehicle brands and models.
* Discover trends in vehicle demand over time.
* Evaluate regional sales performance.
* Identify high-revenue customer segments.
* Analyze factors influencing vehicle purchases.
* Generate insights to support inventory and marketing decisions.
* Build an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders.

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains vehicle sales transaction records including vehicle details, customer information, pricing, sales performance, and geographical data.

### Key Analysis Areas

* Vehicle Performance
* Revenue Analysis
* Customer Insights
* Geographic Analysis
* Sales Trends
* Inventory Optimization

## 🔍 Business Questions Answered

1. Which vehicle brands generate the highest revenue?
